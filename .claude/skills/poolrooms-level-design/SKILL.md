# Poolrooms level design (researched 2026-11-12)

## What the community canon describes
Sources read: https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-37 , https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-37-1 , https://backrooms-pantheon.wikidot.com/level-37 (fan fiction wikis, creative reference only, confidence medium). The fandom wikis (backrooms.fandom.com, escapethebackrooms.fandom.com) refuse automated fetches (HTTP 402): read them in a browser if needed.
- Pristine identical WHITE ceramic tile on floors, walls AND ceilings; blue-green lukewarm water; a constant faint ripple without cause. (L37)
- Rooms and corridors of uniform size next to large IRREGULAR open areas: contrast of scale (sprawling halls vs narrow hallways). (L37)
- MORE PILLARS THAN PRACTICAL rising out of the water. (L37)
- Pools without ledges; STAIRCASES THAT DESCEND STRAIGHT INTO DEEP WATER; underwater holes to darker, deeper parts with poor light and few exits. (L37)
- Light from irregular angles; some areas fully dark. (L37)
- Sound: noises cut off abruptly, wrong reverb, muffled speech; water noise swings between calm and overwhelming. (L37)
- Enclosed dark tunnels as exits; a corridor rarely continuing into an identical corridor elsewhere (seamless portal). (L37)
- 37.1: same geometry, but the lights DIM gradually as the transition cue; viscous red-tinted water; the only light = bright RED growths; distant chanting as a lure; slithering in the darkest corners; a submerged camera as a found-footage prop. (L37.1)
- Fan-wiki zoning (freewriting wiki, via search summary only): Daylight / Twilight / Midnight / Abyss zones going darker and deeper. Low confidence.
- POOLS (Tensori 2024, reviews: https://www.gamespew.com/2024/04/pools-review-a-liminal-hidden-gem/ , https://fingerguns.net/games/2024/05/21/pools-review-pc-the-backroom-buoys/): six short chapters, navigation / small mazes are the challenge, no UI or music, realistic echoes carry the mood. Medium confidence.
- Common Poolrooms imagery (general knowledge of the genre art, unverified): arched doorways, round windows / skylights showing a blue sky, water slides, round tiled tunnels, tiled stairs and platforms at several heights.

## How it maps to this project (decided G19 with the user)
- ONE big pool per floor (the full water system handles one rectangle); every other room is dry tile: hallways, galleries, raised platforms.
- 4-6 rooms per floor, ~5 minutes; spaces: big pool hall + narrow corridors, several heights inside a floor (balconies, platforms, stairs), small set pieces (slide, round tunnel, arches, diving board), a dark / differently tinted zone (37.1 red light) between bright halls.
- Lighting: every lit hall as bright as floor 1 (no glare), darker corridors / zones for contrast.
- Floors are connected by a real ELEVATOR cabin (press E, doors close, ride with hum + shake, doors open on the next floor). The pool hall (default map) leads to floor 1 through its existing north-bank stairs.
- Only boxes: arches = lintel boxes over openings, round tunnels = square tiled tunnels, slides = stepped ramps (terrain ramps only run along z).
